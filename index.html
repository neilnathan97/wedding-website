Wedding Details

Our dream for this wedding is to create an immersive weekend in nature, surrounded entirely by the people we love most. More than just a ceremony, we want this to be a fun, weekend-long retreat where we can all slow down, connect, and play together in the woods.

Throughout the weekend, the property will be ours to explore. We'll fill our days with simple, soulful activities to enjoy at your own pace: guided morning yoga sessions, scenic afternoon hikes through the valley, lazy gatherings at the swimming pond, buffet-style meals, a late-night cozy tea lounge, soaking in the hot tub, storytelling around the campfire, and long nights of dancing.

We would love to reserve the property from Thursday through Monday if enough people are interested in arriving on Thursday. Otherwise, we would reserve the venue from Friday through Sunday. Please indicate in the RSVP form if you'd be interested in arriving early or departing late for a long weekend!

This will be more of a grassroots wedding. We are building this weekend from the ground up, and it is being made entirely possible through the love, help, and unique hands-on support of our close friends and family. From setting up spaces to sharing talents, this weekend will be a collective labor of love, and we are incredibly grateful to have such an amazing, caring community to help bring our vision to life. At the end of the day, spending time together is all that will matter to us!

Getting to Whispertree

Whispertree is tucked into the scenic hills of Boonville within Anderson Valley, roughly 2.5 hours north of San Francisco.

✈️ Flying In

The closest major airports are:

  • San Francisco International (SFO) • ~2.5 to 3 hour drive. Offers the most flight options.
  • Oakland International (OAK) • ~2.5 hour drive. Slightly easier traffic pulling out of the Bay.
  • Charles M. Schulz–Sonoma County (STS) • ~1 hour drive. A smaller regional airport in Santa Rosa with direct flights via Alaska/Avelo.

🚗 Driving & Carpooling

  • Cell Service Note: Service can get spotty as you enter Anderson Valley. We highly recommend downloading offline Google Maps of Boonville/Ukiah before hitting the road!
  • Carpooling: Because we love a shared road trip adventure, we highly encourage grouping up with other guests. Keep an eye out for a carpool coordinator sheet closer to the date!

What to Bring & Wear

🌲 What to Wear

If you know us (which you do, since you’re invited to our wedding), you know our dress code is usually tie dye and bare feet…so consider this our version of dressing up! Our official wedding theme is Lost in the Moss.

Channel your inner whimsical forest nymph. Think earth tones, flowy fabrics, and fun, enchanted details. Feel free to get as fancy as your forest-loving heart desires, but definitely no tuxes or formal gowns are required. Keep it festive, earthy, and comfortable!

  • 🌿 Footwear Note: The ceremony and reception will be outdoors on natural terrain. Please skip the high heels! Unless you want to go barefoot, we highly recommend flats, boots, or sandals so you can explore and dance comfortably.
  • 🌿 Evening Layers: The woods get chilly when the sun goes down! Bring a cozy jacket or sweater to stay warm.

🎒 What to Bring

Get ready for a weekend spent outside in the woods! In late May, the weather is generally sunny, reaching mid-70s during the day and dropping to mid-40s at night. We recommend bringing:

  • 🌿Comfortable walking shoes
  • 🌿Clothes you can hike in
  • 🌿Small hiking backpack for water and snacks
  • 🌿Warm layers for the evenings (it can get pretty chilly!)
  • 🌿Swim suit for the pond and hot tub
  • 🌿Towel
  • 🌿Sunscreen & Sunglasses
  • 🌿Toiletries and medications
  • 🌿Headlamp

⛺ Planning to camp? Bring a tent, sleeping bag, and sleeping pad. If you want to camp but don’t have gear, we have plenty to share!
